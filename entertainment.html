VOX Cinemas · Oman & UAE
VOX Cinemas
Managed social content strategy and copywriting for VOX Cinemas across one mall in Oman and multiple malls across the UAE. The work spanned content ideation, copy in both English and Arabic, collaboration with the creative team, and ongoing recommendations on what to post based on what was hot at the cinema. Content was structured around three recurring pillars designed to drive engagement, build excitement, and convert scrollers into ticket buyers.
Project Details
ClientVOX Cinemas
MarketsOman · UAE
ChannelInstagram
LanguagesEnglish & Arabic
RoleStrategy · Copy · Creative
Content Pillars
🏆
Competitions
Weekly movie-tied competitions - consistently the top-performing posts for engagement. Followers were asked trivia questions based on the current release (e.g. "Name the 3 actors who play Spider-Man to enter") and winners received two tickets to watch with someone they love. CTAs included liking, sharing, and following the page to qualify - turning each post into an acquisition tool.
🎬
Movie Posts
Upcoming film showcases designed to build anticipation and drive footfall. Posts highlighted new releases, premiere dates, and what made each film worth watching - copy calibrated to spark excitement without spoiling anything, giving audiences a reason to show up on opening weekend.
🍿
Generic & Lifestyle
Brand-building content that showcased the cinema experience itself - the ambience, screen formats, and comfort of VOX's different cinema types. These posts reinforced the brand's identity beyond individual films, making VOX the obvious choice for a night out.
✍🏼
My Role
Brainstormed content pillars and the weekly content calendar. Collaborated with the creative team on post concepts. Recommended content based on what was trending at the cinema. Wrote copy in both English and Arabic depending on the target audience and platform requirements.
