Cosmotel 2021
When Cosmotel 2021 took over Dubai's City Centre Mirdif, it brought the pages of Cosmopolitan to life as a high-energy, pastel-hued "beauty hotel." It wasn't just a retail event, it was a full-blown experiential wonderland.

The Programme

Six days of back-to-back masterclasses, brand takeovers, and exclusive events and a new reason to show up every single day.

💄
Beauty Masterclasses
Charlotte Tilbury, NARS, Benefit, Laura Mercier, Kroma Drama, Kiehl's, and Noha Nabil - daily masterclasses hosted by some of the region's most recognised makeup artists and beauty brands.
🛍
Brand Lounge Takeovers
Dyson, Charlotte Tilbury, Foreo, Clarins, Benefit Cosmetics, Molton Brown, and The Body Shop each took over the beauty lounge - daily rotations that gave every visit a fresh experience.
🎤
Talks & Exclusives
A virtual Q&A with Dr Barbara Sturm, a spring/summer trend talk, a fashion presentation featuring H&M, Steve Madden and THAT Concept Store, and a charitable runway inspired by the autism spectrum.
