Game of Thrones meets UAE summer.

Hisense Middle East · Reactive Content · 2019
Winter Has Gone
The final season of Game of Thrones had just aired and the whole world was talking about it. Rather than letting the moment pass, we turned it into a product post. The iconic "Winter is Coming" warning flipped into "Winter Has Gone", tied directly to the arrival of UAE summer and the need for a reliable AC.

The GOT font, the icy creative, and the copy made it feel like a natural part of the cultural conversation, not a forced brand intrusion. It's the kind of post that gets shared because it's clever, not because it's promoted.
